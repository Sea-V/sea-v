Privacy Policy

How SEA-V collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you manage your maritime career with us.

Last updated: 4 October 2026 Contact: admin@sea-v.com Read Terms of Use →
This policy describes how SEA-V handles personal data on our platform. It is written for transparency. Before a full public launch, have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.

01 Who we are

SEA-V is a digital platform for seafarers to manage career records, sea time, certificates, references, and related documents, build a CV, and share an optional public profile. For privacy enquiries or data subject requests, email admin@sea-v.com.

Under UK GDPR (and EU GDPR where it applies), SEA-V is the data controller for the account and career data you provide. The service providers listed in section 07 process data on our behalf as processors.

02 What we collect

Depending on how you use SEA-V, we may process:

  • Account data — name, email address, and login credentials (passwords are held by our authentication provider and never visible to us)
  • Profile data — photo, rank, qualification, nationality, passports and visas held, date of birth, location, availability, career overview, and contact details you choose to save
  • Career records — vessels, sea time, navigation passages, tenders, onboard experience (including captioned photos), milestones and achievements, specialist qualifications, hobbies and interests, references
  • Employment documents — Seafarer Employment Agreements and their addendums, certificates, payslips, reference letters, and other files you upload
  • CV choices — the template, wording, and which items you include on your CV, saved to your account so they follow you between devices
  • Referee details — the name and email address of anyone you ask to verify a reference, and what they enter when they respond (see section 06)
  • Feedback — messages you send through “Report an issue”, with the page and app version they were sent from
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, and error reports processed to keep the service running and secure (see section 07)

03 Sensitive and high-risk data

You may upload financial or identity-related documents (for example payslips, employment agreements, CoC scans, medical certificates). These are private — payslips, salary, leave package, employment agreements and addendums, and certificate files are never shown on your public profile and are only accessible to your own account.

Do not upload documents you are not entitled to store. You are responsible for the accuracy and lawfulness of content you add to your account.

04 Why we use your data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To store and display your career records inside the app
  • To calculate sea time and milestone progress
  • To generate your CV and optional public profile
  • To send reference verification requests you ask us to send
  • To keep the service secure, fix errors, and prevent abuse
  • To respond to support and data protection requests

Our lawful bases under UK/EU GDPR are contract (providing the service you signed up for) and legitimate interests (operating, securing, and improving the platform, and contacting the referees you name). Where required, we rely on consent — for example when you make your profile public. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

05 Public profile (your choice)

Public visibility is off by default. If you enable “Make my public profile visible”, the following may be viewed by anyone with your public link:

  • Shown when public: name, photo, rank, availability, location, nationality, your age (calculated from date of birth — the date itself is never shown), the countries of passports and visas you hold (never document numbers), career overview, vessels and their details and photos, total sea time and sea-time-based milestone progress, navigation passages and map, tenders, onboard experience entries and their photos, certificates (name, issue and expiry dates only), specialist qualifications, milestones and achievements (verified and self-declared, marked as such), verified references, and hobbies and interests
  • Never shown publicly: email, phone, date of birth, salary, leave package, passport and visa numbers, payslips, employment agreements and addendums, certificate numbers, issuing authorities and certificate files, your CV choices, and referees’ email addresses
  • A referee’s CoC (Certificate of Competency) number is never shown — your profile only notes that one is on file
  • You can turn your public profile off at any time in Profile settings

06 References and referees

When you ask SEA-V to verify a reference, we email the referee you name with a single-use link that expires after 14 days. We use their name and email address only to send that request.

If the referee confirms, we store what they enter — their name, rank, reference text, any note, date, signature, and optionally their CoC number. A confirmed reference, including the referee’s name, rank, note, and signature, may appear on your public profile if it is enabled. Their email address and CoC number are never shown publicly. If they decline or ignore the request, nothing is recorded as verified. Referees can contact admin@sea-v.com to have their details removed.

07 Storage, security, and service providers

Your records and files are stored privately under your own account and protected by access rules that let only you read your private data. The service is served over HTTPS.

We use these providers to run SEA-V:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in, and file storage, hosted in London (UK)
  • Vercel — hosts the website, with anonymous page-speed measurement (Speed Insights, no cookies)
  • Resend — sends reference verification emails
  • Sentry — receives error reports when something breaks, hosted in the EU (Germany). Reports are set up to exclude your name, email, and anything you type into forms
  • jsDelivr, cdnjs, and unpkg — deliver standard code libraries the pages use; CARTO and OpenStreetMap — provide map images on navigation maps. Like any website request, these see your IP address but receive none of your records

If a personal data breach occurs that puts your rights at risk, we will report it to the Information Commissioner’s Office within 72 hours and tell affected users without undue delay, as UK GDPR requires.

08 Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account yourself at any time in Profile settings → Delete my account: this permanently removes your records and uploaded files. Anything not removed automatically will be deleted on request, subject to any legal obligation to keep certain records.

09 Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated records
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Complain to a data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office

To exercise these rights, email admin@sea-v.com from your registered address. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10 Cookies and browser storage

SEA-V does not set cookies, and we use no advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies from anyone else. That is why there is no cookie banner.

We do keep a small amount of information in your browser’s own storage, only to make the service work:

  • Sign-in session — keeps you signed in (essential)
  • A copy of your own records — so pages load quickly; cleared when you sign out or close the tab
  • Temporary file links — short-lived links to view your own documents (cleared when you close the tab)
  • Your CV draft — a local copy alongside the one saved to your account
  • Small preferences — for example your recent searches, whether the CV settings panel is folded, and which badge celebrations you have already seen

None of this is shared with third parties or used to track you. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings; you will need to sign in again.

11 Where your data is held

Your account, records, and files are stored in London (UK). Error reports are held in the EU (Germany). Some providers (Vercel, Resend) are US-based companies and may process limited data, such as email addresses for referee emails or website requests, outside the UK. Where they do, it is covered by their data processing terms, which include the safeguards UK GDPR requires for international transfers.

12 Children

SEA-V is intended for professional seafarers and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

13 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
