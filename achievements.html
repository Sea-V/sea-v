Engineering Progression

The Small Vessel engineer tickets (MSN 1904), from MEOL to Chief Engineer. Each shows the Limited to Yachts rule and the full Small Vessel rule; the nearer one is shown. Add each vessel's engine power (kW): it decides what counts.
